Overview

Lightweight on hardware.
Heavyweight on features.

TouchPoint Lite brings the full-fat TouchPoint experience to a portable, tablet-based setup. You get the same reliable software, the same feature depth, and the same offline resilience — just in a form factor that goes wherever you need it.

It's the ideal solution for businesses that can't justify a full fixed terminal in every location, but won't compromise on software quality. Set it up at the bar, take it outside for festival service, or use it as a secondary till during peak hours.
