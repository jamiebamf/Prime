Overview

Take bookings
around the clock.

TouchReservation handles your table booking process from start to finish — automatically. Customers book through your website, receive instant confirmation, and get reminders before their visit. Your team arrives knowing exactly what the day looks like.

It connects directly to your TouchPoint table plan, so every reservation automatically allocates a table, updates availability, and removes the need for manual diary management. No double bookings, no missed reservations.
