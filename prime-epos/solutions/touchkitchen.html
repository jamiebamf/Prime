Overview

Never lose a ticket.
Never miss an order.

TouchKitchen replaces the chaos of paper tickets with a clear, real-time kitchen display. Every order from the till, PocketTouch, or ByTable appears on screen the moment it's placed — colour-coded, timed, and easy to manage.

Kitchen staff mark items as done with a single tap, the display updates, and front-of-house knows the food is ready. No shouting, no lost tickets, no confusion during a busy service.
