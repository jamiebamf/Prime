Overview

Stock management
without the headaches.

TouchStock puts stock management in the palm of your hand. Count stock on the shelf with a handheld scanner, print shelf-edge labels, check supplier pricing, and sync everything directly to TouchOffice Web — without ever going back to a desk.

It eliminates the two biggest causes of stock problems: manual counting errors and delayed data entry. Everything updates in real time, so your back office always reflects what's actually on the shelf.
